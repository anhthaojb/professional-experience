# Versatica BI Dashboard & Alerting

## Overview

A BI product research and testing project focused on dashboard visualization, alerting mechanisms, and BI platform features.

The work involved researching how alert thresholds could be defined for different chart types and evaluating related BI features.

---

## My Role

**Data Analyst / BI Intern**

My responsibilities included feature research, alert mechanism analysis, testing, documentation, and collaboration with Developers.

---

## Research Approach

The research focused on three main questions:

### 1. When should an alert be triggered?

Identify suitable threshold logic based on the visualization and business requirement.

### 2. How should the alert be displayed?

Study how alert conditions could be communicated to users through dashboard visualizations.

### 3. What happens after an alert is triggered?

Research the subsequent notification and alert workflow.
