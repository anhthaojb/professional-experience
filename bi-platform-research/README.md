# BI Platform Research

> Research on modern dashboard and data visualization platforms
> for BI product development.

## Overview

Conducted a structured review of modern BI and data visualization
platforms to identify visualization, analytics, semantic modeling,
AI, embedded analytics, customization, scalability, and security
capabilities that could be relevant to BI product development.

## Research Objectives

The research focused on answering:

- How flexible are chart and dashboard configurations?
- How modern is the visualization experience?
- How strong are semantic modeling capabilities?
- What AI / NLQ capabilities are available?
- How flexible are embedded analytics options?
- How suitable are the platforms for on-premise deployment?
- How well do they support security and row-level access control?
- What are the potential infrastructure and TCO considerations?

## Evaluation Framework

Platforms were evaluated using five major dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Popularity | 15% |
| Modern & AI | 20% |
| Aesthetics | 20% |
| Embedded & Custom | 25% |
| Data Scale | 20% |

## Apache Superset as Baseline

Apache Superset was used as the technical baseline because
the product is built around the Superset ecosystem.

The research therefore focused on identifying areas where
the product could be improved without losing the flexibility
of the underlying architecture.

Areas of particular interest included:

- Dashboard builder UX
- Chart configuration
- Visualization aesthetics
- Semantic / metric management
- AI / NLQ
- Embedded analytics
- Custom visualization
- Permission management

## Key Research Direction

The research suggested that the strongest opportunities for
product improvement are not necessarily replacing the BI engine,
but improving the experience around:

- Visualization configuration
- Dashboard UX
- Governed metrics
- Semantic modeling
- AI-assisted analytics
- Embedded analytics
- Custom visualization
