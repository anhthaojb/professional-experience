# BI Platform Research

## Overview

Conducted a structured review of modern BI and data visualization
platforms to understand their capabilities in visualization,
dashboarding, semantic modeling, AI-assisted analytics,
embedded analytics, customization, scalability, and security.

The research was conducted to identify relevant features,
visualization approaches, and product references that could
support the development and improvement of an internal BI product.

---

## Research Objectives

The research focused on the following questions:

- How flexible are dashboard and chart configurations?
- How modern and intuitive is the visualization experience?
- How strong are semantic modeling and metric management capabilities?
- What AI / Natural Language Query capabilities are available?
- How flexible are embedded analytics and customization options?
- How suitable are the platforms for different deployment scenarios?
- How well do they support security and access control?
- How do they compare in terms of scalability and potential cost?

---

## Evaluation Framework

Platforms were evaluated across five main dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Popularity | 15% |
| Modern & AI | 20% |
| Aesthetics | 20% |
| Embedded & Custom | 25% |
| Data Scale | 20% |

The framework was used to organize the comparison and identify
platforms with strengths relevant to different BI use cases.

## Apache Superset as the Baseline

Apache Superset was used as the main baseline because the
internal BI product is built around the Superset ecosystem.

The research therefore focused not only on comparing BI platforms,
but also on identifying useful ideas and capabilities that could
potentially improve the existing product experience.

Areas of particular interest included:

- Dashboard builder UX
- Chart configuration
- Visualization aesthetics
- Metric and semantic management
- AI / NLQ
- Embedded analytics
- Custom visualization
- Permission management

---

## Research Areas

### Visualization

Compared chart types, dashboard layouts, visual configuration,
interaction patterns, and overall visualization experience.

### Semantic Modeling

Reviewed how different platforms organize metrics, dimensions,
business logic, and reusable analytical definitions.

### AI & NLQ

Explored AI-assisted analytics and Natural Language Query
capabilities across modern BI platforms.

### Embedded & Customization

Reviewed embedded analytics, white-labeling, custom visualization,
and product-level customization capabilities.

### Scalability & Deployment

Considered data scale, deployment models, infrastructure,
and suitability for different operational environments.

### Security & Governance

Reviewed access control, permission models, and governance-related
capabilities relevant to enterprise BI.

---

## Key Takeaways

The research showed that different BI platforms tend to focus
on different strengths:

- Traditional enterprise BI platforms provide mature reporting,
  governance, and analytics capabilities.
- Modern analytics platforms emphasize usability,
  collaboration, and interactive exploration.
- AI-focused platforms emphasize conversational analytics
  and natural-language interaction.
- Embedded analytics platforms focus more strongly on
  customization and integration into other products.
- Visualization-focused platforms provide useful references
  for dashboard aesthetics and user experience.

Rather than replacing the existing BI foundation, the research
was used to identify specific capabilities and design approaches
that could be relevant to future product improvements.
